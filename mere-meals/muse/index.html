Connect Mere Meals to Meta Muse

Ask Muse what's for dinner, have it plan the week, or check the grocery list. Changes show up in the app for everyone in your household right away.

Before you start

  • Sign in to Mere Meals on your iPhone and create or join a household.
  • Have a Muse account.

Connect

  1. In Muse, ask it to add a custom connector for this MCP server:
    https://mcp-meals.mereapps.com/mcp

    For example: “Connect to the Mere Meals MCP server at https://mcp-meals.mereapps.com/mcp.”

  2. A Mere Meals sign-in page opens. Sign in with the same account you use in the app: Apple, Google, or email.
  3. Choose what Muse may do: see your plan, recipes and grocery list, and optionally plan meals and save recipes. Tap Allow.
  4. Muse shows the connection in its side panel. Back in the chat, ask away.

Things to try

  • “What's for dinner tonight?”
  • “Plan tacos for Thursday dinner.”
  • “Find a chicken recipe we've saved and put it on Sunday.”
  • “Swap Tuesday and Wednesday's dinners.”
  • “What do I need to buy this week?”

Muse can see your meal plan, saved recipes and grocery list. If you allowed it, it can also plan, move and remove meals and save new recipes. It can't change your account or your household.

Privacy and disconnecting

What Muse reads from your household is sent to Meta and handled under Meta's privacy policy, not ours. See the Mere Meals privacy policy for what we store.

To disconnect, remove the Mere Meals connector in Muse. Deleting your Mere Meals account also ends the connection.

Other assistants

The same address works with any assistant that supports remote MCP servers with sign-in. Add it wherever that assistant lists its connectors.
