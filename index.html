Mere Apps

Software that does one thing well. No bloat, no upsells, no feature creep. Just simple tools that stay out of your way.

Philosophy

Most software tries to do too much. Every update adds features nobody asked for, buries the thing you actually need behind three menus, and turns a simple task into a decision tree.

Mere Apps exist because the best tool is the one you don't have to think about. Each app solves one problem, does it cleanly, and stops there.
